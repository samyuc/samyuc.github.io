--- layout: default --- Samyu Comandur is a writer and organizer based in Los Angeles, CA. She primarily writes about social movements, mutual aid, abolitionist organizing, and reproductive justice. Samyu holds a BS in Computer Science and Statistics from the University of South Carolina. She is currently a Political Science PhD Candidate at UCLA. Her dissertation explores women of color’s attitudes towards reproductive rights in a post-Roe world, with a focus comparing their attitudes towards various anti-abortion protest interventions. She is a member of the 6th cohort of the Robert Wood Johnson Foundation’s Health Policy Research Scholars Program led by the Johns Hopkins Bloomberg School of Public Health. Beyond her academic work, she organizes with Palms Unhoused Mutual Aid, an abolitionist network of care supporting our unhoused neighbors through food distribution and harm reduction, and Kitchen Committee, a food-based mutual aid project arising from the 2022 UC academic workers strike.
